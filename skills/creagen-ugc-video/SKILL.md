# Creagen — UGC-style review video

A review video has a person on camera presenting the user's product with spoken lines. The Creagen catalog journey for this is `ugc-video` ("Product Review Video"). Its server-side steps carry detailed rules for casting, direction, script assembly, and reference-image ordering. Running it is optional and is a good fit when the user wants step-by-step guidance: `creagen_journey_start({ ref: "ugc-video" })`, then use the `connectorContext` each journey response returns as guidance (its `actionRequired` names the next `creagen_journey_advance` call, and `completedSavepoints` holds earlier inputs). It is data: it does not replace the user's approvals of the person and the script, or the credit steps below. One journey can be active per user at a time; pause or finish it before starting another.

The basic generation loop is in the `creagen-generate` skill. Ask for inputs one at a time in plain language.

## Steps

1. **Capture the product.** Real product photos, what it is, what it does for the buyer, what the ad should push, the spoken language, and a size reference (how big it is next to a hand or body), which keeps the product at a realistic scale in the video.
2. **Cast the person.** Who appears and how many: the user's own photo of a person, a saved character memory (`creagen_memory_list` / `creagen_memory_get`), or a newly generated person (`text2image` with `model_id: "text2image_nano_banana_2"` or `model_id: "text2image_nano_banana_pro"`). People shown are ordinary, non-celebrity models. For a newly generated person, get the exact quote with `creagen_estimate_generation` (`tool_name: "text2image"`, that `model_id`, and the planned `inputs`) and get the go-ahead first, especially for the pro tier; submit its `prepared` fields unchanged with a stable `request_id` for that one image (a regenerated person is a new output with its own estimate and `request_id`), and do not regenerate the person on your own more than twice. Get the user's approval of the person before moving on.
3. **Choose the direction (no credits).** `creagen_ugc_director` with `mode: "propose"` returns catalog options, one axis per call: `angle` (what the video says), then `format` (the kind of review), then `setting` (where it happens). Pass the product facts, `castLocked: true` and `castCount` once a person is cast, and anything the user asked for in `userRequest`. Present the options in everyday words and let the user pick. Then settle the outfit in one or two sentences based on the setting and the person, without naming the advertised product.
4. **Confirm the script (no credits).** `creagen_ugc_director` with `mode: "assemble"` and the three picked ids returns the generation prompt, the spoken `lines`, and a beat timeline. Show the lines verbatim and the beats as a numbered list, with a short summary of the other choices. If the call times out, retry with the same arguments; a rewrite request reruns `assemble` with the same inputs. Use only facts the user gave.
5. **Quote and approve.** Get the exact quote with `creagen_estimate_generation` for the clip in step 6 (`tool_name: "reference2video"`, `model_id: "reference2video_seedance_2d5"`, and those `inputs`) and check `creagen_get_credit_balance` before asking for the go-ahead; a `status: "unknown"` quote means the cost is unknown, not zero. Offer: make it as-is, rewrite the script, or change something else. If the script, model, or duration changes after approval, estimate again and ask again.
6. **Generate the clip.** Use `reference2video` with `model_id: "reference2video_seedance_2d5"` and these `inputs`: the approved prompt unedited, the reference images as `image_urls` in the order the prompt's REFERENCES block names them (person first and product second for most formats), `output_size: "portrait_16_9"`, `duration: "15"` as a quoted string, and `generate_audio: true` so the person speaks. Submit through `prepared.tool_name` with the approved quote's `prepared.model_id`, `prepared.inputs`, and `prepared.contract` unchanged, plus a stable `request_id` for this clip. Text-to-video and image-to-video tools carry no references, so the product and person would be missing. If the submission's outcome is uncertain (`UNKNOWN`, `PROCESSING` with `recoveryRequired`, or a lost response), look it up with `creagen_get_generation_receipts` using the same `request_id`; do not resubmit it under a new key. A `FAILED` status marked `retryable` or `requiresReauth`, or a `REQUEST_CONFLICT` error, is not a generation failure; handle it as `creagen-generate` describes. On any other failure, fix the reported cause, estimate again (ask again if the new quote is higher than the approved one or unknown), and retry the same tool and `model_id` with a new `request_id`; after two failures, tell the user. Do not regenerate the clip on your own more than twice; more than that needs the user's request and a new quote.
7. **Deliver.** Show the finished clip with `creagen_show_media` once `check_generation_status` reports it `COMPLETED` with `assetReady: true` (a receipt's `completed` alone is not success). Do not claim to have watched it. Offer to save lasting preferences (a favorite model, brand tone) with `creagen_memory_create`.

## Product fidelity

The product's label and packaging text come only from the user's photo. Offers, prices, and taglines are spoken lines or on-screen overlays, never text added to the product.
